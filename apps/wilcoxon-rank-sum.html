This applet computes probabilities for the Wilcoxon Rank Sum Test. The Wilcoxon rank sum statistic $W_1$ is the sum of the ranks in the first sample.

Directions

  • Enter $n_1$ and $n_2$ (total $n \le 30$ for exact pmf).
  • The plot shows the PMF of $W_1$ under the null hypothesis.

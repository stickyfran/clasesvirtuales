This applet computes probabilities for the Wald–Wolfowitz Runs Test.

Directions

  • Enter the number of events of type 1 ($n_1$) and type 2 ($n_2$).
  • The plot shows the PMF assuming $H_0$ (randomness) is true.

Select the tail and enter $x$ to find the p-value or specific probability.

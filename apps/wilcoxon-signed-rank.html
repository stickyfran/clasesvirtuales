This applet computes probabilities for the Wilcoxon signed rank test. The Wilcoxon signed rank statistic $W$ is the sum of the ranks of the absolute values of the positive observations.

Directions

  • Enter the sample size $n$ (max 20 for exact calculation).
  • The plot shows the probability mass function (pmf) under the null hypothesis.
